## 1. Purpose and Scoring Scale
**Clause 1.1** - Score 1: Foundational - Applies the competency focus only with step-by-step guidance; experience is limited to training exercises or shadowed tasks, and the deliverables produced need substantial rework.
**Clause 1.2** - Score 2: Developing - Applies the competency focus to well-defined tasks with regular review; the deliverables produced meet basic standards but miss edge cases and context.
**Clause 1.3** - Score 3: Proficient - Independently applies the competency focus to typical problems; the deliverables produced are dependable, documented and accepted by peers without major rework.

**Clause 1.5** - Score 5: Expert - Sets direction for the competency focus across the organisation; the deliverables produced become standards, and the person measurably raises the capability of other teams.
