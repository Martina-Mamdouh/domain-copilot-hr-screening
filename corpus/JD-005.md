## 4. In-Depth Competency Specifications & Behavioral Indicators
### 4.1 Competency: Customer Focus (CUS)
**Definition:** Orientation toward the needs of users and customers, evidenced by research, feedback loops and decisions that visibly improve their experience.
**Target Expectation:** Adapts understanding and serving users and customers to ambiguous or cross-team problems; the research findings and improvements produced are reused by others, and the person coaches peers on the approach.
**Observed Behavioral Indicators:**
- Seeks direct user input before and after delivery
- Translates feedback into prioritised change
- Balances customer requests against product direction
- Handles complaints with empathy and follow-through

## 6. Structured Interview Probes
**Customer Focus Probes:**
- Tell me about a time user feedback changed your plan.
- Describe how you handled an angry customer or stakeholder.
- How do you decide between what users ask for and what they need?
**Communication Probes:**
- Give an example of explaining a complex topic to a non-expert.
- Describe a document you wrote that changed a decision.
- Tell me about a misunderstanding you caused and how you repaired it.
**Data Literacy Probes:**
- Describe a time the data contradicted your expectation.
- How do you validate a dataset you did not create?
- Tell me about a metric that turned out to be misleading.
**Leadership and Mentoring Probes:**
- Describe someone you developed and what you did differently for them.
- Tell me about an unpopular decision you made and how you handled it.
- How do you decide what to delegate and what to keep?
**Ownership and Delivery Probes:**
- Describe a commitment you nearly missed and what you did.
- Tell me about something you owned after it went live.
- How do you decide what not to do?
**Collaboration and Teamwork Probes:**
- Tell me about a disagreement with a colleague and how it ended.
- How do you hand over work so the next person can continue it?
- Describe a time you changed your approach to suit the team.
