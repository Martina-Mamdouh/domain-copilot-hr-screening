## 6. Structured Interview Probes
**Technical Depth Probes:**
- Walk me through the most technically demanding piece of work you delivered and what you would change today.
- How did you choose between two viable technical approaches, and what evidence did you use?
- Tell me about a time a technical assumption of yours proved wrong in production.
**System Design Probes:**
- Describe a design you owned. Which alternatives did you reject and why?
- How did you decide where a system boundary should sit?
- Tell me about a design that did not survive contact with real traffic.
**Code Quality and Testing Probes:**
- Show me how you decide what to test and what not to test.
- Describe a review comment that changed your mind.
- How did you reduce flaky tests or regressions on a past team?
**Problem Solving Probes:**
- Tell me about a problem where the first diagnosis was wrong.
- How do you structure the investigation of an intermittent failure?
- Describe a trade-off you analysed and how you decided.
**Ownership and Delivery Probes:**
- Describe a commitment you nearly missed and what you did.
- Tell me about something you owned after it went live.
- How do you decide what not to do?
**Collaboration and Teamwork Probes:**
- Tell me about a disagreement with a colleague and how it ended.
- How do you hand over work so the next person can continue it?
- Describe a time you changed your approach to suit the team.
